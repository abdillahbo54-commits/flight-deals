# Regional tips

Search tips by region, not prices: every price still comes from a tool.
Apply the general rules to every route, then the matching region section.

## General rules (all routes)

- **Peaks**: school holidays, Christmas / New Year, summer, and religious or
  national holidays at either end (Eid, Diwali, Lunar New Year,
  Thanksgiving…). Holidays on a lunar calendar move each year: check the date
  before advising. In a peak: say so in one line, check ±3 days and mid-week
  departures, and note that waiting is risky (see `price-alerts.md`).
- **Baggage**: low-cost fares often include only a small under-seat bag.
  Mark those rows "checked bag not included" unless the tool says otherwise.
  If the tool gives a bag fee, show price + bag as the total. Never guess a fee.
  Travellers visiting family usually need a checked bag: make it the
  clarifying question.
- **Alternate airports**: use city codes (`iata-codes.md`), plus one extra
  query on a realistic nearby airport (same region). Say what it changes
  (ground transfer time and cost).
- **Two one-ways** (different airlines) vs a round trip: one extra query.
- **Open-jaw** (in one city, out of another) when the user tours a country.
- **Hub connection**: a cheaper flight via a big hub (Istanbul, Doha, Dubai,
  Madrid, Lisbon, Frankfurt…) can beat a direct flight. Flag long layovers,
  airport changes and **self-transfer** (separate tickets) as risks.
- **Transit visas**: some connections need a transit visa depending on the
  passport. Flag the question, check an official source; never assume.

## Europe (intra-Europe)

- Dense low-cost network (Ryanair, easyJet, Wizz Air, Vueling, Transavia,
  Volotea…), often from secondary airports: Paris BVA, Brussels CRL, London
  STN / LTN, Milan BGY, Stockholm NYO. The ground transfer can erase the gain.
- Trains compete under ~4–5 h (Paris–London, Paris–Amsterdam, Madrid–
  Barcelona): mention it as an option, without prices.

## Maghreb ↔ Europe (Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia)

- Peaks: summer (strongest in July outbound from Europe, late August back),
  Christmas, Eid al-Adha and the end of Ramadan.
- Many travellers carry 20–23 kg: bag is often decisive.
- Regional airports often have direct flights from Europe: Morocco (TNG,
  NDR, OUD, RAK, AGA, FEZ), Algeria (ORN Oran, CZL Constantine), Tunisia
  (DJE Djerba, MIR Monastir, NBE Enfidha). CMN, ALG and TUN are the main hubs.
- Southern Spain (AGP, SVQ) and southern France (MRS, MPL, TLS) are useful
  alternates. Ferries are outside this skill: mention them, no prices.

## Europe ↔ North America

- Peaks: summer, Christmas, Thanksgiving (US).
- Compare direct vs one stop via Dublin, Reykjavík, Lisbon, Madrid or a
  European hub. Basic-economy transatlantic fares may exclude a checked bag
  and seat choice: flag it.
- Multi-airport cities matter: NYC (JFK / EWR), WAS, CHI, YTO, LON, PAR.

## Middle East and Gulf

- Gulf hubs (DXB, DOH, AUH) and Istanbul (IST) connect Europe, Africa and
  Asia: often good value with one stop, sometimes with long layovers.
- Peaks: Ramadan end, Eid, summer, Hajj and Umrah seasons for Saudi routes
  (JED, MED).

## Africa (Sub-Saharan)

- Fewer direct routes, higher fares. Compare connections via Casablanca,
  Addis Ababa, Nairobi, Istanbul, Paris, Brussels, Lisbon or the Gulf.
- Peaks: Christmas / New Year and summer (diaspora travel).
- Bags and long layovers matter a lot; flag self-transfers strongly.

## Asia

- Long-haul: compare Gulf / Istanbul connections with European or Chinese
  hubs. Inside Asia, strong low-cost networks (AirAsia, Scoot, IndiGo,
  VietJet…) with strict bag rules.
- Peaks: Lunar New Year (whole region), Golden Week (Japan, China), Diwali
  (India), local school holidays.
- Multi-airport cities: TYO (NRT / HND), SEL (ICN / GMP), BKK (BKK / DMK),
  SHA, BJS.

## Latin America

- Hubs: Madrid and Lisbon from Europe; Miami, Panama City, Bogotá, São
  Paulo, Mexico City inside the Americas.
- Peaks: Christmas / New Year, Carnival, Easter, July holidays.
- Multi-airport cities: SAO (GRU / CGH / VCP), BUE (EZE / AEP), RIO, MEX.

## Oceania

- Long-haul from Europe: one or two stops via Asia or the Gulf. Compare
  total duration, not only price.
- Peaks: Christmas / southern summer, Easter, school holidays.
